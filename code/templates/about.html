What is Anti-Money Laundering (AML)?

Anti-Money Laundering (AML) refers to the policies and procedures that financial institutions and other regulated entities implement to prevent, detect, and report suspicious activities related to money laundering.

Why is Anti-Money Laundering Important?

Money laundering is a significant financial crime that involves disguising illegal financial activities as legitimate. It can have serious consequences for the economy and financial institutions. By implementing AML systems, we can safeguard against illicit financial activities, including drug trafficking, terrorism, and corruption.
